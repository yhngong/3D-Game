# Aethelgard: Realms of the Astral Forge (3D Voxel Action-RPG)

A 3D Minecraft-inspired voxel sandbox Action-RPG built with **Three.js + TypeScript + Vite** featuring a **completely original 6-Tier Progression System** (no Netherite, no Ender Dragon), **5 custom multi-phase bosses**, **8 custom 3D monsters**, and **mythic melee, ranged, and magic weapons with right-click abilities**.

---

## 🌟 6-Tier Custom Progression System

1. **Tier I: Timber & Flint (Verdant Groves)**
   - Harvest *Ancient Oak Logs*, *Sylvan Leaves* (*Living Vines*), and *Cobblestone* (*Razor Flint*).
   - Craft the **Bramble Thorn-Blade**, **Sylvan Hunter Shortbow**, **Flint Pickaxe**, and **Bramble Hide Tunic Set**.
2. **Tier II: Verdant Bronze & Sylvan Core**
   - Mine **Copper Ore** & **Tin Ore** and smelt **Verdant Bronze Ingots**.
   - Hunt **Sylvan Briar-Stalkers** and **Ancient Moss Golems** for *Sylvan Essence*.
   - Craft the **Verdant Effigy** to summon **Boss I: Sylvan Behemoth, Ancient Treant Titan** (drops *Heart of the Grove*).
3. **Tier III: Frostforged Mythril (Permafrost Peaks)**
   - Mine glowing **Frostforged Mythril Ore** and **Cryo Crystal Clusters**.
   - Battle **Cryo-Crystal Crawlers** and **Permafrost Wraiths**.
   - Forge **Frostforged Mythril Armor** (unlocks **Double-Jump**!), **Frostforged Greatsword**, and **Cryo-Repeater**.
   - Craft the **Permafrost Idol** to summon **Boss II: Cryonax, The Permafrost Titan** (drops *Cryo Matrix Core*).
4. **Tier IV: Solar Pyroclast (Scorched Magma Wastes)**
   - Mine volcanic **Pyroclast Ore** and gather **Solar Cinders** from **Pyroclast Magma Brutes**.
   - Forge the **Pyroclast Inferno Scythe**, **Magma Cannon**, and **Solar Pyroclast Warplate** (grants Magma immunity).
   - Craft the **Cinder Sigil** to summon **Boss III: Ignis Rex, The Molten Colossus** (drops *Heart of Ignis Rex*).
5. **Tier V: Void-Touched Aetherium (Astral Void Rifts)**
   - Harvest gravity-defying **Aetherium Rift Ore** and slay teleporting **Abyssal Void Reavers** for *Void Crystals*.
   - Forge the **Voidrend Dimensional Katana** (with *Shadow Blink Strike* teleport slash!), **Aetherium Arc-Railgun**, and **Voidwalker Armor** (grants slow-fall Astral Glide).
   - Craft the **Void Resonator** to summon **Boss IV: Malakor, Architect of the Void** (drops *Astral Keystone*).
6. **Tier VI: Celestial Starsteel (Final Ascension)**
   - Mine **Celestial Starsteel Ore** and fuse **Celestial Starsteel Ingots**.
   - Wield the **Zenith Starforged Blade** (fires piercing cosmic crescents + *Celestial Starfall* meteors), **Chronos Astral Scepter** (homing supernova comets), and **Celestial Starsteel Regalia** (Starlight Flight).
   - Craft the **Astral Zenith Crown** to challenge the Final Boss: **Boss V: Aethelgard, The Astral Sovereign**!

## 🎮 Controls

- **W, A, S, D**: Move / Strafe
- **Space**: Jump / Double-Jump / Hold in mid-air to Glide (Tier V–VI Armor)
- **Shift**: Sprint
- **Q**: Directional Shadow Dash
- **Left Click (Hold)**: Mine Voxel Blocks / Swing Melee Weapon / Fire Ranged Weapon
- **Right Click**: Activate Weapon Special Ability / Place Selected Block / Use Potion or Boss Summon Relic
- **1 – 9** or **Mouse Wheel**: Select Hotbar Slot
- **E**: Open Astral Forge (Crafting) & Inventory
- **P**: Open 6-Tier Progression & Boss Codex (includes Sandbox Kit & Quick Boss Summon buttons)
